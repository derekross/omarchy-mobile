# Pixel 8 Pro (husky) roadmap: from "boots" to an everyday phone

Written 2026-10-31 by Lef ([@Somnius](https://github.com/Somnius)). Last updated: 2026-11-07. The original roadmap for this port stops at "boots" ([issue #1](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/issues/1)), and that bar is met on the units tested so far: Omarchy runs from slot B and unlocks by touch. This page is the rest of it, one row per subsystem, in the order of the columns in [docs/DEVICES.md](../../docs/DEVICES.md). For each it says what works, who is on it, the closest finished work to copy from (the Pixel 9a, `tegu`, has a newer Tensor SoC (zumapro) than husky's zuma [I: neighbouring generations, so device-tree work should carry over in part], and the Pixel 7 Pro, `cheetah`, has a plan for almost everything), what is in the way, and a first step that is read-only (no flashing). Credits are at the end.

Facts are marked [V] (checked, with the source) or [I] (an inference, with what would settle it). Pull request states are as of 2026-11-07. "Merged" means on `main` of this repository or on `omamobile` of the kernel; everything else ran only on a test tree, and the table says which pull request it waits on. Where a boot of Lef's test tree is the evidence, the kernel release was `7.3.0-rc6-omamobile-push1-g50ef3c279021` for most rows and a later test tree, `7.3.0-rc6-omamobile-push1-ge98a7a38a3ef`, for NFC (the test branches and tags are on his fork and listed in [AOC-PLAN](AOC-PLAN.md)). Nothing here asks anyone to skip the safety rules: read-only first, one change per boot, a read-only backup of the modem and calibration partitions before radio, PMIC, AoC or Trusty work ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) §1, [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) §3). Kernel PR numbers are in [derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile), written `kernel #N`.

## What works today

Two units are in play: Brett's (Samsung 128 GB UFS 3.1) and Lef's (Kioxia 256 GB UFS 4.0; see [NOTES.md](NOTES.md)). Results below say which unit unless both did them. Kernel: `omamobile` plus Brett's `husky-test-20261031-c6` tag for the 2026-10-31 results; since then Lef's phone has run test trees made of Brett's open PRs plus the ones listed below.

**On `main` (merged or landed):** boot, UFS, USB networking, the watchdog, the framebuffer display with its panel size (kernel #154), touch (kernel #153), buttons (kernel #148), the hsi2c_9 and hsi2c_11 buses with the gauge, the PMIC and the pack EEPROM (kernel #163, #175; the buses still need a bring-up overlay until kernel [#205](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/205)), the `splash` command line ([#278](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/pull/278)) and the device package's display start, USB replug rescue and retain-splash ([#228](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/pull/228), [#250](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/pull/250), [#285](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/pull/285)).

**On test trees only (open PRs):** the DRM display at 120 Hz (kernel #160), the GPU (kernel #157), CPU DVFS and thermal zones (kernel #158), Wi-Fi (kernel #156), Bluetooth (kernel #199), haptics and torch (kernel #200), the AoC with speakers and microphone (kernel #203, #204, package #453, [AOC-PLAN](AOC-PLAN.md)), the Type-C sink and the charger under a dead-man tool ([#272](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/issues/272), draft [#282](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/pull/282)).

- **Boot** from slot B, with stock or GrapheneOS on slot A as the way back. Slot B is deliberately not marked successful by any package, so ABL gives it three boots ([BRINGUP-M1](BRINGUP-M1.md), [UFS-PLAN](UFS-PLAN.md)). On 2026-11-01 Lef's phone ran out of retries and GrapheneOS wiped `userdata` on its next boot ([BATTERY-NOTES](BATTERY-NOTES.md) §9); from 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-07 a local unit marked slot B on this phone; stopped pending [#252](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/issues/252).
- **UFS** at HS-G4, two lanes, behind the read-only guard, on both device vendors ([UFS-PLAN](UFS-PLAN.md) U2 passed on the Kioxia unit).
- **USB gadget** networking and SSH, and the watchdog driver (no 80 s reset).
- **Display** as a simple framebuffer on `main`: console, lock screen and the shell at 1344x2992, with the panel's physical size (70x155 mm) since [kernel #154](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/154) (merged 2026-11-01), so the on-screen keyboard has its proper height (196 px, 21% of the screen, instead of 105 px) [V: measured on the Kioxia unit]. On a test tree with [kernel #160](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/160) (open): gs-decon, DSIM0 and the HK3 panel at 120 Hz with brightness, started by the device package ([#228](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/pull/228), merged); Lef's phone has run it since 2026-11-01.
- **Touch** (STMicroelectronics SALIXP over SPI) and **buttons** (`gpio-keys`): raw events decoded in the right corners, swipe, PIN pad, app launching, wake by tap and by Power (both units). Merged 2026-10-31 as [kernel #153](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/153) and [#148](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/148).
- **Foundations those PRs added:** the ALIVE pin controller, the CMU_TOP and CMU_HSI0 clocks, the ACPM mailbox and protocol driver, the main PMIC (S2MPG14), USI2 and its SPI; then CMU_PERIC1, USI9 and USI11 with the battery buses ([kernel #163](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/163), [#175](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/175), merged 2026-11-01).
- **Battery and charging** on `main` plus an overlay: the gauge with Google's ATL model, the PMIC, the pack EEPROM; the charger driver by hand under a dead-man tool; the Type-C controller driver as a sink (at the Type-C 3 A level, replug survives) in the same guarded runs ([BATTERY-NOTES](BATTERY-NOTES.md), [issue #1](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/issues/1#issuecomment-6562547503)). Host mode with VBUS out waits for the runbook review ([#272](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/issues/272), draft [#282](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/pull/282)).
- **Wi-Fi, GPU, CPU DVFS and thermal zones** on test trees with Brett's open kernel PRs [#156](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/156), [#157](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/157) and [#158](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/158): on Lef's phone 5 GHz Wi-Fi, panthor on the Mali-G715 and cpufreq with the four ACPM TMU zones ([NOTES](NOTES.md) for Wi-Fi; the GPU and DVFS results are in those PRs, not re-measured here).
- **Bluetooth** (BCM4398D0 on the APM UART): pairing and A2DP headphones, on [kernel #199](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/199) (open); firmware from the owner's own vendor image.
- **Haptics and torch** (CS40L26, LM3644): felt and seen, on [kernel #200](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/200) (open).
- **Sound**: the AoC firmware loads and runs (GSA, Trusty, System MMU v9, mailboxes, 94 services), both CS35L41 amplifiers probe with the speaker-protection firmware from the owner's vendor image, and the AoC sound card plays stereo through the speakers and records from one microphone, through UCM2 and PipeWire. Test tree only: [kernel #203](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/203), [#204](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omarchy-mobile/pull/204), package [#453](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/pull/453), plan and results [AOC-PLAN](AOC-PLAN.md) (all open). The AoC core port and the System MMU v9 file are not PRs yet.

Touch and Buttons are in `docs/DEVICES.md` with their merged PRs; the rest of this list is "testing" or "WIP" there, with the PR it waits on.

A proposal, not a verdict; Brett and Derek know the hardware better.

3. **Display driver** (Brett): works at 120 Hz on #160; it needs a second phone's test and review.

## How to help without risking a phone

- Read Google's `android-gs-shusky` trees and the stock device tree for each row's chips, and write the missing `*-PLAN.md` in the cheetah shape: status, facts with [V]/[I] and their source, design, a gated runbook, results.
- Test other people's branches on a second unit and say exactly what ran ([CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) §6.5). That is how the touch, button and display-size results here were produced.
- Review pull requests against [docs/review/LESSONS.md](../../docs/review/LESSONS.md).
- Everything that boots a phone follows the staged rules: read-only first, a log stream to the laptop before touching hardware, one change per boot.

## Open questions

- ~~Which Bluetooth, NFC and haptics parts does husky have, and which GPU generation?~~ Answered above: BCM4398D0 (#199), ST54L (stock tree), CS40L26A and LM3644 (#200), Mali-G715 on panthor (#157). The zuma `clk-acpm` variant is in #157.
- Which microphone id is which hole, and what do the earpiece and headset paths need (#450, #453)?
- Why does the phone draw about 0.5 A idle with the screen off, and why did the CPU idle-state and power-domain overlays reboot-loop it? Not known; no measurement plan yet.
- In which file shape should the zuma System MMU v9 code and the AoC core port go (asked on #1)?
- Marking slot B successful (a `devinfo` write): decided on [#252](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/issues/252) (2026-11-07). It is a manual, attended step, once per install of a smoke-tested image, never from a unit, with per-phone layout evidence first. The boot LUNs get the whole-disk node removed and each partition's own flag set first (its own PR, booted on the maintainer's Pixel 7 Pro), then the marking tool ([#483](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/pull/483)). Until both are in nothing marks slot B on the 8 Pro: flash a known-good set before the boot that would use B's last try, and count every deliberate reset as a boot. On Lef's phone a local unit marked it from 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-07, after it ran out of retries once; stopped pending #252. On Lef's phone slot B stayed marked (`devinfo` byte 0x35 read `06`) from the unit's last write until the first flash after the unit was masked on 2026-11-07, when it read `04`. Since then, about a dozen flashes on 2026-11-07 (each followed by `fastboot set_active b`; a `flash`, which also sets B active, clears the bit) have read `slot-retry-count:b` as 3 after `set_active` and 2 after one boot, so a boot spends one of B's tries and the flash-before-the-last-try routine above applies. `slot-successful:b` was not read after those flashes, so "cleared" rests on the retry count and byte 0x35, not on the bootloader's own answer. The bit is not cleared by hand: that would be a `devinfo` write too.
- Does touch survive a long idle with the screen off? One 12 h idle on 2026-11-04 left only Power working; not reproduced, not cause-checked.
