# Supported devices

The status of each device, as verified on real hardware. Rows in the matrix have merged notes behind them; ports still in review are listed under [Wanted](#wanted) with their issue or pull request. What's still missing and the order we plan to tackle it: [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). To add a device or update a cell, follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) §6.4: only claim what you've run on your own phone, and link the notes entry that shows it.

**Legend:**

- **works**: verified on the phone, on by default;
- **testing**: works by hand, not yet on by default;
- **partial**: works on by default, with a known gap that the notes name;
- **WIP**: bring-up in progress, some gates passed;
- **built**: driver and DT written and built, not yet run on the phone;
- **boots**: the block's firmware runs, but nothing user-facing uses it yet;
- **no**: not started, or blocked;
- **-**: the hardware doesn't exist on this device.

| Cameras | works (telephoto fixed-focus) | Ultrawide (Sony IMX386, D-PHY link 2): works end to end through our CSIS/WDMA driver and libcamera's software ISP (CAM5), basic quality (no Google ISP or tuning); a libcamera build with autofocus and low-light patches is installed, not yet evaluated (CAM5.3). Front (Samsung S5K3J1, D-PHY link 0): real images, raw and through libcamera (`cam -c1`, 30 fps, no WDMA errors; F.5-F.7, 2026-10-13). Main (Samsung S5KGN1, C-PHY link 1): 2016x1136 at 30 fps through libcamera with no WDMA errors. Telephoto (S5KGM5, C-PHY): 26 fps through libcamera, colour bars correct; its lens (SEM1215) isn't driven yet, so it stays at its resting focus: distant subjects are sharp (outdoors, 2026-10-26), close ones soft, and tap to focus does nothing. The CSIS write DMA (not the link) loses lines that arrive faster than about 400 MB/s within a line, so the sensors lengthen their lines to stay under `samsung,max-line-throughput` (360 in cheetah's DT for the main, telephoto and front cameras; the front's full 3840x2736 mode runs at 26 fps). The ultrawide (imx386) follows the same rule, so its full 4032x3016 mode streams (23 fps; one run in several still showed WDMA errors), and with 384 MiB of CMA `cam` without options runs every camera at full size (main 12 MP at 9.4 fps). The telephoto's lens (SEM1215) answers on a 950 kHz bus but holds the bus when driven longer, so the telephoto runs at fixed focus. Sensor and DC-PHY settings come from the user's own camera software (`tools/extract-camera-data`), never shipped. Since omamobile-device-cheetah 0.1.30 the camera modules load after boot (`omamobile-camera.timer`), the DMA-BUF heaps are open to the session (libcamera's software ISP), WirePlumber names them Main, Ultrawide, Telephoto and Front camera (0.1.31). The default camera app is omacam ("Camera", omamobile-apps 0.2.0; [CAMERA-APP-PLAN](CAMERA-APP-PLAN.md)): libcamera directly, zoom across the three back lenses, full-resolution photos. GNOME Snapshot is optional, named "Snapshot", for video until omacam records it; browsers and video calls use the cameras through PipeWire (`pipewire-libcamera`). Picture quality: libcamera 0.7.2-4.21 adds chroma noise reduction and defective pixel correction, and omacam 0.3.3 has Night mode and an HDR chip, bursts of raw frames aligned, merged and finished on the phone (Night: 12 frames in about 4.6 s, Q5.3 on 2026-10-21); single shots are still well below stock's | [CAMERA-PLAN](../device/cheetah/CAMERA-PLAN.md), [CAMERA-QUALITY-PLAN](CAMERA-QUALITY-PLAN.md) |
| Suspend | WIP | s2idle works when asked for (`systemctl suspend`, `rtcwake`): `pm_test` dry runs, wakes on a volume key and on the RTC alarm, 20 of 20 unattended cycles (S3b, S4); UFS powers down in suspend by default (`spm_lvl` 5). Wi-Fi stays connected across sleep: 31 of 31 freezes with the D3 handshake (S5b-3, S5c, 2026-10-19), and the sleep hook keeps it loaded. The AoC and sensors work across sleep, and a running sensor stream doesn't wake the phone (S6, 2026-10-19). Screen off, with Wi-Fi up, it draws 434 mA awake and 312 mA in s2idle: about 16 h of standby on the 4986 mAh battery (S7, 2026-10-19), against 32-50 mA in stock's deep sleep. Not on by default: the phone doesn't sleep by itself when the screen goes off until S8 (Power to sleep and wake, idle sleep, a day of use) has run. Wi-Fi traffic and a USB plug can't wake it, a few RTC cycles wake early on a PMIC interrupt not yet identified, and the modem has never run across a suspend. Deep sleep (SYS_SLEEP), which stock's standby numbers need, is research and not started | [SUSPEND-PLAN](../device/cheetah/SUSPEND-PLAN.md) |
