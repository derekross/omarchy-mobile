# Supported devices

The status of each device, as verified on real hardware. Rows in the matrix have merged notes behind them; ports still in review are listed under [Wanted](#wanted) with their issue or pull request. What's still missing and the order we plan to tackle it: [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). To add a device or update a cell, follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) §6.4: only claim what you've run on your own phone, and link the notes entry that shows it. Status as of 2026-11-24.

**Legend:**

- **works**: verified on the phone, on by default;
- **testing**: works by hand, not yet on by default;
- **works (opt-in)**: verified on the phone and started by the device's package once the owner turns it on, off by default for a reason the notes name;
- **partial**: works on by default, with a known gap that the notes name;
- **WIP**: bring-up in progress, some gates passed;
- **built**: driver and DT written and built, not yet run on the phone;
- **boots**: the block's firmware runs, but nothing user-facing uses it yet;
- **no**: not started, or blocked;
- **-**: the hardware doesn't exist on this device.

## Shared hardware

Phones on the same Tensor chip share its blocks: CPU, GPU, UFS storage, USB, the AoC audio and sensor processor and the PMICs. Work done on one phone's chip side usually carries over to its siblings. Board parts are per phone: the display panel, touch, cameras and the battery pack differ even between siblings, and every phone has its own device tree, its own pins and its own AoC board id. So a feature that works on one phone is not marked as working on its sibling until it has run there; this section only shows where the hardware is the same, so a port knows where to start.

- **Tensor G2 (gs201):** Pixel 7 Pro (cheetah), Pixel 7 (panther), Pixel 7a (lynx), Pixel Fold (felix). The Pixel 7 uses the same parts as the 7 Pro for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth (BCM4389), haptics (CS40L26A), the torch (LM3644), GNSS (BCM4775), the speaker amplifiers (two CS35L41), the fingerprint sensor (Goodix G7) and the charger and gauge (MAX77759); its panel (S6E3FC3) and touch (FT3658) differ. The Pixel 7a has a Qualcomm Wi-Fi and Bluetooth chip (WCN6855), its own panel (S6E3FC5) and touch (GT9895). Sources: the board device trees `gs201-cheetah.dts`, `gs201-panther.dts`, `gs201-lynx.dts` and `gs201-felix.dts` in [linux-omamobile](https://github.com/derekross/linux-omamobile/tree/omamobile/arch/arm64/boot/dts/exynos/google), and [panther's fingerprint plan](../device/panther/FINGERPRINT-PLAN.md).
- **Tensor G1 (gs101):** Pixel 6 Pro (raven), Pixel 6 (oriole), Pixel 6a (bluejay). The buttons, gauge and USB-C controller come from one shared device tree, `gs101-pixel-common.dtsi`; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are BCM4389 on all three. The Pixel 6 and 6a both have a Samsung S6E3FC3 panel (each its own variant) and an ST FTS touch controller of the same family; the Pixel 6 Pro's panel (S6E3HC3) and touch (S6SY792) differ. Sources: [oriole's notes](../device/oriole/NOTES.md) and [bluejay's notes](../device/bluejay/NOTES.md).
- **Tensor G3 (zuma):** Pixel 8 Pro (husky), Pixel 8a (akita), Pixel 8 (shiba). The 8 Pro and 8a share the charger, gauge and USB-C controller (MAX77759, on the same bus and addresses: [akita's notes](../device/akita/BRINGUP-M1.md)); their panels (HK3, AK3B) and touch (FTM5, GT9895) differ, and the 8a's battery pack has its own model.
- **Tensor G4 (zumapro):** Pixel 9a (tegu); the Pixel 9 Pro XL (komodo) and 9 Pro Fold (comet) have board device trees in review. Their chip-side features will start from the 9a's.
- **The same part across families:** Synaptics S3908 touch (Pixel 7 Pro, Pixel 9a), CS40L26A haptics (Pixel 7 Pro, 8 Pro, 9a), LM3644 torch (Pixel 6 Pro, 7 Pro, 8 Pro, 9a), GT9895 touch (Pixel 7a, 8a), ST FTM5 touch (Pixel 8 Pro and the Fold's cover screen; the Pixel 6a's ALIX is the same family and the same driver), MAX77759 charger and gauge (Pixel 6, 6 Pro, 6a, 7, 7 Pro, 8 Pro, 8a), BCM4389 Wi-Fi and Bluetooth (Pixel 6, 6 Pro, 6a, 7, 7 Pro), Goodix G7 fingerprint (Pixel 7 Pro, 7, 9a), BCM4775 GNSS (Pixel 7 Pro, 7, 8 Pro). The driver carries over; each phone still needs its own device-tree node.
